What We Do

HEIGHT IS OUR WORKSPACE, SAFETY — OUR PRIORITY

Oritoki Ltd. is a rope access company providing safe, fast and efficient solutions in locations where scaffolding, cranes or other traditional access methods are impractical or ineffective.

Our team consists of experienced mountaineers, rock climbers and rope access technicians.

With over 15 years of experience, extensive technical knowledge and international practice, we are equipped to carry out complex and non-standard projects with a high level of precision and in accordance with established safety standards.

Our team members hold internationally recognized IRATA, SPRAT and IFMGA Rope Access certifications.
