What We Do

HEIGHT IS OUR WORKSPACE, SAFETY OUR PRIORITY

Oritoki is an industrial rope-access company that delivers safe, fast and effective works at height where the use of scaffolding, cranes or other traditional methods is impossible or ineffective.

Our team brings together mountaineers, rock climbers and certified works-at-height specialists with many years of hands-on experience.

We have been operating in this field for over 18 years. The technical knowledge and international experience accumulated over the years allow us to carry out projects of any complexity with the highest precision and safety.

Our work is based on the IRATA, SPRAT and IFMGA international rope-access standards, ensuring the highest level of safety, quality and professionalism.

Our core values are safety, responsibility, precision and client trust. We carry out every job with modern equipment, in compliance with international standards, and with accountability for the result.
